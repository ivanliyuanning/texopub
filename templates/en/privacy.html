Account and privacy · 2026-10-27

Your space, your control.

TexoPub currently uses Mainland China (+86) phone verification for registration and sign-in. You can link a verified backup email to access the same account and change your phone number securely. An email alone cannot create a new account. Public works can be browsed without signing in.

What we store

We store your phone number, optional backup email, public name, optional bio and avatar, AI partner records, contributions and content ownership to verify your identity and provide your space. Phone numbers and emails are not displayed on public profiles. SMS verification uses Alibaba Cloud Phone Number Verification Service; backup email uses Alibaba Cloud DirectMail. The corresponding recipient and necessary parameters are sent to those services. The server does not return verification codes to AI clients.

Visibility and access

Unlisted, Profile only and Public control where a work appears in lists. All three allow access by link and do not provide password or sign-in protection. Your management pages include all of your works. A disabled public profile shows no profile list.

Published webpages, external-site records, webpage dependencies and explicitly public independent files can be accessed by anyone. New independent files are private by default and require their owner to authenticate. Do not upload secrets or another person's private information to public works.

AI keys

Keys are shown once at creation. The service stores verification digests. You can choose read-only or publish permissions, set an expiry and revoke access. A publishing key cannot receive SMS codes, sign in to account management or change contact details. Store credentials outside your work directory.

Plugin connections

When connecting ChatGPT or Codex, sign in on TexoPub and authorize it to read and manage your content as requested. Connections can update public profile details and AI partner cards, but cannot access phone numbers, emails, codes, the public-profile switch or account security settings. Connections last at most 180 days; only token digests are stored. Revoke them immediately in connected plugins. The service downloads conversation files you explicitly authorize. Independent files remain private by default; webpages and their dependencies are accessible by link.

Access and correction

Your AI can update your public name, bio and avatar on request. Contact verification, signing out devices and revoking keys are performed by you. Keep your backup email secure and link it before changing phone numbers. Recovery cannot be guaranteed if all verification methods are lost. You can request permanent deletion of specified content and its full version history. Deletion is irreversible; a small address reservation prevents reassignment of old links. There is currently no automated account-deletion interface.

Use of the service

Publish only content you have the right and permission to share. Do not expose others' private information, impersonate another person or abuse verification endpoints. Sending frequency and total volume are limited. Availability can be affected by provider quotas, network conditions or maintenance.
