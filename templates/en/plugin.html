Your webpage is ready.
Let your AI publish it.

In a conversation connected to TexoPub, say “Publish this webpage to my TexoPub space.” The service receives your static HTML and assets and returns a shareable short URL. Updates keep the same address.

Connect the beta

The remote publishing service is available through a personal MCP connection. It is not listed in the public OpenAI plugin directory. Custom plugin availability and mobile support depend on your account and workspace.

  1. In ChatGPT plugins, add a custom MCP service.
  2. Use the name TexoPub and MCP URL https://texopub.com/api/mcp.
  3. Choose OAuth with dynamic client registration (DCR). No client secret is required.
  4. Sign in on TexoPub, check your space and allow the connection. Enter verification codes only on TexoPub.
  5. Select TexoPub in a new conversation and ask it to publish your completed static webpage.

The package contains a publishing Skill and remote MCP configuration, with no credentials or local scripts. Downloading it does not install it or grant access.

What you can ask it to do

  • Publish static HTML with images, styles, scripts and subpages.
  • Find your works, update them at the same URL or list an existing website.
  • Update your name, avatar, bio and AI partner cards, and record actual contributions to a work.
  • Upload independent files with Unlisted, Profile only or Public listing settings. All links work without signing in.
  • Change listing visibility, or permanently delete a work and all historical versions to reclaim storage.

Webpages and their dependencies are accessible to anyone with their links. New works require a valid category and a dedicated icon. Creating a page is not permission to publish it.

One-step publishing accepts up to 8 files, 25 MiB per file, 32 MiB total and 2 MiB of HTML. Staged uploads support up to 250 files and 100 MiB total, committing only when all files pass validation. The service accepts HTML text and authorized conversation files; it cannot read arbitrary paths on your phone or computer. Export ZIP contents or framework projects to static files first.

Your space, your control

Webpage and independent-file listings can be Unlisted, Profile only or Public. All three have accessible links; the setting controls which lists include the work. You can ask your AI to change the default for future works. Existing works keep their visibility.

Connections can manage your works, materials, public profile and AI partner cards. They cannot access your phone number, email, verification codes, profile-publication switch or account security settings. Connections last at most 180 days and can be revoked immediately. Revoking a connection does not delete published works.

Updates use version conflict checks and retries use idempotency keys. All published webpage and independent-file links work without signing in; Unlisted, Profile only and Public control list placement only.
